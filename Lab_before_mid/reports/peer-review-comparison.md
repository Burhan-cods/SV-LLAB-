# Peer-Review Comparison

## Purpose
Compare my defect log with a classmate's log, identify missed defects, and calculate detection rate.

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Defects found by me (A) | 20 candidate findings |
| Defects found by classmate (B) | Fill after exchange |
| Common findings (A ∩ B) | Fill after comparison |
| Total unique findings (A ∪ B) | Fill after comparison |

## Formula
Detection rate = `|A| / |A ∪ B| × 100%`

## Example only
If I find 20 defects and the union contains 25 unique defects, detection rate = 20 / 25 × 100% = 80%.
