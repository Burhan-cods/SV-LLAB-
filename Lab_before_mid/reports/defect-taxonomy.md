# Defect Taxonomy

| Category | Definition | Example from S1-S12 |
|---|---|---|
| Ambiguity | A statement has multiple plausible meanings. | S5: “any requesting party” does not specify the required authorization. |
| Vagueness | A statement uses unclear terms without measurable limits. | S6: “safe temperature.” |
| Incompleteness | Necessary information is missing. | S9 does not define “low viability.” |
| Contradiction | Requirements cannot both hold in the same circumstances without an exception. | S7 limits temperature to -18°C, while S8 permits warming to 0°C during maintenance. |
| Non-verifiability | Compliance cannot be objectively tested as written. | S12: “good audit trail.” |
| Redundancy | Information is repeated without a clear need. | S6 and S7 both address temperature safety, though S7 is more specific. |
| Infeasibility | A requirement may be impossible or impractical to satisfy as written. | S7’s “never” wording may be impossible to guarantee during every equipment failure. |
| Untraceability | The requirement has no clear source, rationale, or link to another artifact. | S2 gives no measurable capacity target or rationale for “large number.” |

These are initial review examples. Confirm each finding against the course interpretation before final submission.
